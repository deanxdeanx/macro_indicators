Write a comprehensive equity research note on this company from the latest earnings release, the quarterly report, and management guidance. Give any documents uploaded with this request more weight than the snapshot, the prompt, or general knowledge. If a figure is not in those sources, write "n/a" instead of estimating it. Do not invent NTM earnings, a price target, or a consensus number.

Cover all of the following, in this order:

1. Business overview and segments. What the company sells, how the segments are organized, and what management said changed this quarter.
2. Discounted cash flow. State every key assumption in a table (growth, margins, reinvestment, discount rate, terminal value). Then show a sensitivity table of value per share against the discount rate and the terminal growth rate. If the cash-flow history is too short for a serious DCF, say so and still show the arithmetic with the assumptions labeled as assumptions.
3. Comparable-company valuation. Name the peer set, the multiples used, and why each peer is or is not a fit. Show the implied range. Use "n/a" where a multiple is not available.
4. Bull, base, and bear cases. One paragraph each, with the single assumption that moves the case.
5. Risk factors. Include the ones management emphasized and any that the filing's own risk language flags as new.
6. What to watch or confirm at the next earnings call. A short list of questions tied to guidance, segments, and the assumptions above.

Keep the tone plain. Separate sourced facts from assumptions.
